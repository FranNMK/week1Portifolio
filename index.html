A Quick Introduction

Before I got into web development, I worked in logistics for about two years. The job was fine, but I kept feeling like I wanted to make things — not just move them. One evening I helped a family member build a basic webpage for their small business and stayed up until 3 AM without noticing. That was the sign I needed.

I enrolled in a structured bootcamp shortly after. This portfolio is my Week 2 submission — four pages of hand-written HTML, no shortcuts.
